When I started my degree in Computer Science at the University of Verona, I knew nothing about programming. I had never written a single line of code.
Three years later, I find myself fluent in a language I once didn’t understand, not just coding but thinking computationally.

The program immersed me in mathematics, physics, and logic before moving into algorithms, software engineering, networks, operating systems, databases, and signal processing.
Each subject opened a new layer of reasoning: from abstract algebra to system design, from the deterministic world of logic to the probabilistic nature of AI and data.

Beyond classes, life in the student residence shaped much of who I became. Surrounded by curious minds and diverse perspectives, I learned as much from people as from books.
Two close friends walked the whole path with me: countless nights of debugging, discussing, laughing.
It was more than a degree. It was a transformation from curiosity into craft.

⸻

## Curriculum Highlights

- **Foundations:** Mathematics & Physics, Logic & Discrete Structures, Probability & Statistics  
- **Systems:** Algorithms & Data Structures, Operating Systems, Computer Networks  
- **Software:** Databases & SQL, Software Engineering, Signal Processing & AI  

> This journey turned curiosity into craft and gave me the confidence to keep building.
