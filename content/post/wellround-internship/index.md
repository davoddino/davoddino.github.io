During my internship at Wellround I contributed significantly to the development of their platform from backend to frontend. I helped build LLM-based agents, the dashboard for university/ student management, the APIs for server ↔ mobile app communication, and the PostgreSQL database schema and queries.
Working across the full stack, I gained hands-on experience with agent design, API architecture, data modelling, and production-ready systems in a startup environment.

My role included:

- Designing and implementing REST/GraphQL endpoints to connect mobile clients and dashboard interfaces.
- Developing and integrating LLM agents that assist students, analyse data, and provide insights.
- Modelling the PostgreSQL schema for students, universities, sessions, analytics, and managing migrations and performance tuning.
- Building the dashboard UI for administrators to manage users, view analytics, and trigger workflows.
- Ensuring end-to-end data flows were secure, efficient, and scalable as the platform grew.

The internship taught me how to transform research-inspired ideas into working services, how to collaborate with product and design teams, and how to operate in a fast-paced startup setting.
